In August 2025, I stepped away from the lab for an unforgettable volunteering trip to Weishan, Yunnan, sharing China's intangible cultural heritage with local students. I hosted hands-on workshops in traditional bamboo weaving and guided energetic Yi ethnic folk dance sessions. Watching the students' faces light up as their first creations took shape was pure magic! Beyond research papers, this experience proved that real impact comes from human connection—reminding me that knowledge and skills are always at their best when shared with others.
